⚑ HAND-OFF FLAGS The tab is called Teas. bottomNavHTML() ships Home · Teas · Log · Sessions · Insights (steep-core.js:894–903), so every board says Teas. Renaming it to “Library” is a separate open decision — flagged, not assumed, and it would touch the nav, this header, and the hand-off's prose together. Vessels is a segment, not an overlay: goView('vessels') → goVessels() sets state.teaSeg='vessels'; state.view='teas' (v3.46). The segment row draws that shipped truth; #05 Vessels rev 1 is the content behind it. #23 gates the shelf controls: setTeaSort / setTeaFilter are held (reinstate-vs-accept). No sort/filter control is drawn here — if they're reinstated, the header's second row is where they land. Decide before build. Vendors is restyle-only: string-based for R3 — rename rewrites teas.source across matching teas; no vendor entity, no url, no logos. Shipped Settings copy already reads “Manage vendors from the Teas tab”, so this is continuity. Data (checked): 21 teas, 2 running low (Honey Oolong Gui Fei 7 g · Sencha Kagoshima Premium 8 g); vendor names and counts are illustrative (R27) pending the vendor slice of the export — the mechanism is real, the tallies are not yet checked.
