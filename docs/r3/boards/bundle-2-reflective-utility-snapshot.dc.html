DATA · FOR CODE The map reads the same three-tier model as the script: a tea plots only when the catalog carries a region with coordinates (Tier 1). No verified region → it simply doesn't plot (Tier 3), never a guessed pin — the “Origins” count stays honest. Blends with no single origin (Earl Grey) are named in a quiet “not from one place” footnote, not forced onto the map. Geometry is Natural Earth via d3-geo; region pins are catalog lon/lat.
SOCIAL

A concept to react to — not finished screens

You asked for the idea first, and you're right that today's social doesn't fit a calm diary. So this is a rethink of what social is for, with small fragments to make it concrete — not a screen set. React to the shape; the screens come after.

THE PRINCIPLE Correspondence, not broadcast. Social in SlowCup is small, deliberate, and pull, not push. It's about the tea moving between people who trust each other's palate — like passing a friend a page of your diary, or lending a good book — never a performance of your activity. Nothing pings you; you visit when you want to.
