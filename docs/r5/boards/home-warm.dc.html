1b · THE DOOR, IN CYCLE DRAWN AT BAND WIDTH
Yes — a register can read as a door It needs three things, and none of them is an edge. The destination in words ("why, on Insights") — the strongest affordance available, and the only one that also says where the tap lands. A chevron at the trailing edge, at the sentence's optical centre. A pressed wash across the full register, so the tap is answered edge-to-edge. The grammar is already on the surface: Home's day rows open session detail, and Running low's rows open a tea — every row on Home is a door already. What the lead insight needs is to be the loudest door, not a different kind of thing, and loudness comes from the chevron and the named destination.

WHAT IS BEHIND THE DOOR The deep page on Insights, where a retrospective is allowed to be one: the profile the claim rests on, the top five it ranks, and the spread behind the ranking. Home carries the one sentence; Insights carries the argument. Same split as Wrapped — moment here, record there. THE DEEP PAGE ITSELF IS AN INSIGHTS SLICE, NOT DRAWN HERE. THIS BOARD RULES THE DOOR, NOT THE ROOM.
WHAT THE TREATMENT ASSERTS The swatch is data: the named tea's liquor. An insight that names no tea carries no swatch and the sentence takes the full width — colour never arrives as decoration. The chevron does not move when the swatch goes, so the door stays the same door through the whole cycle. Present tense, one clause, no second clause commenting on the first.
