Paper, rule, band, slab

One .card ×119 radii is not a frame, it is a default. The system below replaces it with four containers and one law, and the law is the part that matters: the frame never carries a fill. Paper, line and type only — every fill on screen belongs to a rationed mark (liquor, clay, xanthous, blue). That is the lock stated as something you can check by looking, and it is why the radius vocabulary can carry the rationing contracts instead of decorating them.

WHY THIS IS THE SPINE, NOT A STYLE Both asymmetric radii are already rationed by contract — one to identity, one to the single committing action. So a torn corner anywhere else is not a taste question, it is a contract breach, visible at a glance and checkable in a fence. UNREAD IN THIS LANE Four things this board draws and does not know: --clay, --xanthous-wash and --enso hexes; the washi band's real texture; the shipped --paper-adjacent tone, if one exists at all beyond --white #FFFEFB; and the masthead's torn radius values.
