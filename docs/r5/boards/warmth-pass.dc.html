R5 · THE WARMTH PASS · FOUR UTILITY SURFACES DRESS, DO NOT RE-FRAME · CONTAINERS ARE SETTLED

One warm thing

Home shipped warm and the other four surfaces did not, so the app currently has a visible seam: an identity floor with colour on it, and four austere utility floors behind it. This pass closes the seam. Nothing here re-frames anything — every BAND, RULE, BOX and SLAB stays exactly as the spine slices shipped it, and no surface's container count moves. What changes is that the app's own colour arrives on surfaces that had almost none of it: the twelve-stop liquor ramp used at a size you can read, on the rows that actually name a tea. Insights is the priority — it is the surface Niklas reads as a spreadsheet, and it is the foundation the reflection deep pages get built on next, so it takes the most work here. The rule that governs all four: colour arrives as data — a swatch is the tea's liquorFor, so a row with no tea behind it stays plain and gains no placeholder.

1b · THE COLOUR CLOCK THE ONE NEW MECHANIC
The distribution and the tea are one fact The mechanic lands on "When you brew" — brewingClockHTML, the surviving clock, and the only hour graph on the surface since slice 4 dropped the hero's bars. Its twelve 2-hour bars carry when you brewed in one jade. They have room for a second fact at no extra ink: what you were drinking then. Each bar takes the liquor of the tea most brewed in that slot, so a morning of sencha reads pale green and a late shou reads near-black — the shape of your day and its colour in the same mark. This is the strongest warmth available on Insights precisely because it is not decoration: remove the colour and you lose information. It is also the surface's answer to "boring data" — the ledger cannot be made pretty, but the chart beside it can be made true and vivid at once.
